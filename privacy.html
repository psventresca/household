Privacy: Finance receipts

Finance receipts is a private tool used by one person, the owner of this site, for their own household budget. It is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission it reads, read-only, receipt emails from Apple, Venmo and PayPal in the owner's own Gmail account, to label the owner's own bank transactions. It cannot send, change or delete email.

What it reads stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own encrypted, password-locked page. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or sent to anyone else. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
